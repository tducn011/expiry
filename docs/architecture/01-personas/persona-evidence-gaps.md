## Analysis

For P1: reconstruct forgotten-food incident, where stored, how noticed, planned purchase and preparation delay; observe capture before storage.

For P2: recent changed-meal incident, which information/actions were missing, who moved food physically; observe choosing food from attention/detail and explain choice.

For P3: observe adding five entries, partial consume, discard, recount and later physical audit; measure taps/time/corrections/staleness.

Recruit by behavior rather than age/device. With consent, record transcript/source and distinguish participant quote from persona prose. Exploratory small samples identify mechanisms; do not extrapolate prevalence. Baseline manual task first, then OCR only if hypothesis merits study.
